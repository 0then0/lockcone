# Code of conduct

LockCone welcomes contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels. This
code applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces, and to
people representing the project publicly.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people respectfully and make room for different experience and viewpoints.
- Keep technical disagreements focused on behavior, evidence, and tradeoffs.
- Give constructive feedback and respect a person's request to stop an interaction.
- Protect other people's privacy and confidential information.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, personal attacks,
doxxing, and repeated disruption are not acceptable.

## Report a concern

Contact the maintainer, [0then0](https://github.com/0then0), privately at
[zheltov_pe@mail.ru](mailto:zheltov_pe@mail.ru). Include links, a description of
what happened, and any relevant context. Do not publish another person's private
information in an issue or pull request.

Reports are handled as confidentially as practical. Information may be shared
with people needed to investigate or address the concern.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, ask a participant to change
behavior, restrict participation, or exclude someone from project spaces.
Responses depend on the severity and recurrence of the conduct. Maintainers are
also expected to follow this code.
